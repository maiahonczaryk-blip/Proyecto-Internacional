One of the biggest myths among North American buyers is: "Since I don't live in Europe or have a Spanish credit score, I have to pay 100% cash." Not true!

🏦 How Non-Resident Mortgages Work in Spain (via UCI):

  • Up to 70% Financing (LTV): You only put down 30% + closing costs, keeping your capital working for you.
  • Evaluated on Your US / Canadian / PR Income: Qualifying is based on your North American tax returns, W-2s/1099s, and Equifax/Experian/TransUnion credit report.
  • Attractive European Interest Rates: Historically lower than US investment property mortgage rates, with fixed and mixed-rate options up to 25–30 years.
  • 100% Remote Pre-Approval: Get pre-approved before you even step on a plane.

💶 Transparent Numbers: What Are the Closing Costs in Spain?

During Thursday's webinar, we will show you real financial sheets so you never face hidden costs. As a rule of thumb, buyers in Spain budget an additional 12% to 13.5% on top of the purchase price:

• Transfer Tax (ITP on Resales) or VAT (IVA on New Builds): 10% in the Valencian Community (Costa Blanca)
• Notary, Land Registry & Stamp Duty (AJD): ~1.5% – 2%
• Independent Legal Representation: ~1% (includes NIE, bank account, due diligence & closing)
