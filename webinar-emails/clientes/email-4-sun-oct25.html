Buying a home in another country can feel intimidating if you don't know the rules. Unlike North America—where title companies and escrow agents handle closing—Spain uses a strict Civil Law Notary & Land Registry system combined with independent real estate lawyers.

🛡️ Your 5-Step Ironclad Buying Roadmap in Spain:

STEP 1 · Buyer Discovery & Curated Property Tours

As your dedicated Buyer's Advisors at RE/MAX Inmomás, we search the entire market (resales & new developments) and arrange live video tours or VIP in-person visits.
STEP 2 · Legal Due Diligence (Nota Simple & Urban Check)

Before you commit a single dollar, our bilingual legal partners (Fuster & Associates) audit the Land Registry (Nota Simple), town hall permits, and HOA certificates to guarantee zero liens, zero debts, and 100% legal compliance.
STEP 3 · NIE Tax Number & Spanish Bank Account

Every foreign buyer needs a Spanish Foreigner Identification Number (NIE). Your lawyer obtains your NIE and opens your Spanish bank account—even without you having to fly to Spain.
STEP 4 · Private Purchase Contract (Contrato de Arras)

Locks in the property and price with strong legal protections (and mandatory bank guarantees on all off-plan payments).
STEP 5 · Public Deed Signing Before a Spanish Notary

Sign the Escritura Pública in person or remotely via Power of Attorney, receive your keys, and have all utilities & taxes automatically set up for you.
