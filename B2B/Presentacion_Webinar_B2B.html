Block 5 · Q&A · 46:00 – 52:00
Questions You're Thinking Right Now
💬 Do I need a Spanish real estate license to receive a referral fee?
No. The fee is paid as a B2B service agreement — not a commission split. Most brokerages permit international referrals. We provide full legal documentation to your Broker upon registration.
💬 What if my client decides not to buy?
Zero cost. Zero risk. The referral fee is paid only upon successful closing. No retainers, no upfront fees — ever. Your only investment is 5 minutes to register.
💬 How does my client pay in euros from the US or Canada?
Via currency specialists (Wise, OFX) — low fees, competitive rates. Or through UCI's euro-denominated financing. We guide your client through every step.
💬 What taxes does a buyer pay in Spain?
Resale properties: ITP 8–10%. New builds: VAT 10% + 1.5% stamp duty. All taxes are factored into every client's budget from the very first consultation — no surprises.
💬 Can Canadian clients qualify for the Golden Visa?
Absolutely. A 500,000 € + investment grants full EU family residency for parents and children. Processing takes 3–6 months with a 98% success rate through Fuster & Associates.
💬 How does my client know they are legally protected?
Fuster & Associates conducts full due diligence on every property — title search, debts, urban compliance. Every purchase goes through an independent Spanish notary. Legally bulletproof.
