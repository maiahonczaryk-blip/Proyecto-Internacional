Block 5 · Q&A
The Questions You're Thinking
💬 Do I need to be licensed in Spain to receive a referral fee?
No. The fee is paid as a B2B service agreement — not a commission split. International referrals are standard practice. Most brokerages permit this. We provide documentation to confirm.
💬 What if my client decides NOT to buy?
Zero cost to you. Zero risk. The referral fee is paid only upon successful closing. No retainers, no upfront fees — ever.
💬 How does my client pay — in euros from the US?
Via international currency specialists (Wise, OFX) or through UCI financing. We guide clients step by step. No surprises on currency conversion.
💬 What taxes does a buyer pay in Spain?
Resale: ITP transfer tax 8–10% (varies by region). New builds: 10% VAT + 1.5% stamp duty. All factored into client budgets from day one — zero surprises.
💬 Can Canadian clients get the Golden Visa?
Absolutely. €500K+ investment = full EU family residency for Canadians and Americans alike. Fuster & Associates handles the complete application. 3–6 months processing.
💬 How do I know my client is legally protected?
Fuster & Associates conducts full due diligence on every property: title deed, debts, urban compliance. Every purchase goes through an independent notary. 30+ years protecting international buyers.
